02 / How we build

Small team.
Clear principles.

Whatever the topic, every Vatani Labs app follows the same four rules.

01

Offline first

On a rig, at a mine or on a plane: the answer should not depend on a signal.

02

Simple and focused

One clear purpose per app, no clutter, readable at any text size.

03

Accurate content

Carefully prepared, checked against sources, and improved with every report.

04

Privacy respected

No unnecessary data. What you write stays on your phone unless you choose otherwise.
