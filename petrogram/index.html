App 01 / Oil & gas

PetroGram

The offline pocket reference for oil & gas professionals and students: calculators, a 500-term glossary, quizzes, maps, market data and interview preparation, in seven languages.

App Store · coming soon Google Play · coming soon

Inside / Features

Everything in one app.
Online or offline.

Free with ads, or ad-free with a single one-time purchase. No account needed.

Converter & calculators

Units, drilling, production and well-control calculators, with history and favourites.

Glossary of 500 terms

Clear definitions with related terms, plus flashcards with spaced repetition.

Quizzes and exam mode

600 questions by topic, a question of the day, streaks, badges and an optional scoreboard.

Companies and world map

Who owns whom, country by country, on an interactive map.

Offline maps

Basins, fields, pipelines, refineries and active drilling rigs, even without signal.

Market and rig counts

Oil and gas prices and Baker Hughes rig counts, saved for offline use.

Interview preparation

Real questions with model answers, marks and practice by topic.

Cheat sheets & events

Field-ready summaries, HSE guides and the industry calendar.

Seven languages

English, Azerbaijani, Russian, Arabic, Spanish, French and Simplified Chinese.
