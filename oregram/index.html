Inside / Features

Mine your business.
Online or offline.

From the first ore grade to the next interview: the tools and knowledge of geology and mining, always in your pocket.

Calculate

Converter and calculators

Units with ore grades, troy ounces and carats, and calculators for tonnage, grade, stripping ratio, recovery, NSR and NPV.

Learn

600 terms, as flashcards

A glossary of geology and mining with drawings and related terms, and spaced repetition that remembers for you.

Practise

Quizzes and interview preparation

Practise by topic and keep a daily streak, then go through real interview questions with model answers.

Read

Mining, processing, geology, safety

Clear sections from the rocks and the drillhole to crushing, flotation and smelting, and mine safety.

Explore

Offline maps of mines and deposits

Mines, plants and mineral deposits from official sources, and coal by country, with no signal needed.

Who is who

Companies by country, with a map

Mining companies and public bodies in 39 countries, and where they are on a world map.

Follow

Monthly metal and mineral prices

Gold, silver, copper, iron ore, coal and more: monthly averages from the World Bank, saved so you can read them offline.

Understand

Seven languages

English, Azerbaijani, Russian, Arabic, Spanish, French and Simplified Chinese, fully translated.

Anywhere

No signal? No problem.

Underground, at a remote site or on a plane: everything important works offline.
